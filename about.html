Our Heritage & Purpose

Pioneering World-Class Medical Diagnostics in Botswana

Gerald Holdings was established in Gaborone with a singular, uncompromising vision: to ensure that patients and referring physicians in Southern Africa never have to travel abroad to access benchmark diagnostic imaging. By investing in German-engineered Siemens Healthineers 3T MRI and 128-Slice CT machinery, combined with Deep Resolve acceleration, we have redefined diagnostic precision in our region.

ENVIRONMENTAL, SOCIAL & GOVERNANCE (ESG)

Empowering Citizens & Protecting Our Environment

Gerald Holdings believes that technological leadership must go hand-in-hand with social responsibility and environmental stewardship.

🌱

Zero-Chemical Dry Imaging & Solar Power

By transitioning to 100% cloud PACS archiving and dry laser printing, we have eliminated over 10 tons of toxic silver halide photographic developing chemicals and wastewater annually. Furthermore, our Gaborone HQ integrates solar PV backup to reduce grid carbon footprints.

🎀

Rural Breast Cancer Screening Campaigns

Every October during Breast Cancer Awareness Month, Gerald Holdings sponsors free 3D tomosynthesis mammography and ultrasound screenings for hundreds of underprivileged women across remote villages in partnership with the Ministry of Health.

Trust, Privacy & Clinical Use

Clear responsibilities for a trusted digital service

Privacy notice

Information submitted through booking, referral and training forms is used to respond to the request, coordinate service delivery and meet legitimate clinical or administrative obligations. Access should be restricted to authorised personnel. Patients may contact Gerald Holdings to request access, correction or appropriate deletion, subject to legal and clinical record duties.

Terms of clinical use

Website information is educational and does not replace personalised medical advice. Portal demonstrations and sample records are illustrative. Imaging suitability, protocols and results must be determined or explained by qualified healthcare professionals. In an emergency, use the appropriate emergency service rather than a website form.

Data protection & security

The platform is designed around data-minimisation, encryption in transit, role-based access, auditability and privacy-aligned workflows. Production deployment requires HTTPS, controlled staff access, retention rules and formal governance under applicable Botswana data-protection and healthcare requirements.
