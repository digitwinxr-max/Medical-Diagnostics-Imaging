IMAGE QUALITY & CLINICAL GOVERNANCE

Radiologist-Led Diagnostic Excellence

Every examination follows a disciplined pathway—from protocol selection and image acquisition to technical quality review, consultant interpretation and a signed clinical report.

01

Protocol-led acquisition

The examination is tailored to the referral question, patient history and relevant safety screening.

02

Technical quality assurance

Radiographers verify coverage, motion, positioning and image quality before the study proceeds to reporting.

03

Consultant radiologist review

A qualified radiologist interprets the complete study, correlates relevant prior imaging and signs the final report.

⚖️ Clinical responsibility

Final clinical interpretations are made and signed by qualified radiologists. Results should be discussed with the referring healthcare professional.
