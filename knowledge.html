PREPARATION GUIDES

Simple steps for a smoother appointment.

Your booking confirmation may contain protocol-specific instructions. If those instructions differ from those below, follow the booking confirmation.

🧲

MRI preparation

  • •Remove jewellery, watches, cards and removable metal.
  • •Tell staff about implants, devices, pregnancy or prior metal injury.
  • •Advise us about claustrophobia before the appointment.
  • •Fast only if your booking instructions say contrast or sedation is planned.
⭕

CT preparation

  • •Follow the fasting instruction supplied for contrast studies.
  • •Tell staff about allergies, kidney disease, diabetes and current medicines.
  • •Bring relevant blood results if requested.
  • •Hydrate after contrast unless a clinician has restricted your fluids.
🌊

Ultrasound preparation

  • •Pelvic studies may require a comfortably full bladder.
  • •Abdominal or liver studies may require fasting.
  • •Wear comfortable clothing and bring prior imaging.
  • •Obstetric instructions vary by stage of pregnancy.
🎀

Mammography preparation

  • •Do not apply deodorant, powder or lotion to the chest or underarms.
  • •Bring previous breast images and reports if available.
  • •Tell the technologist about symptoms, surgery or pregnancy.
  • •Wear a two-piece outfit for convenience.
☢️

Digital X-ray

  • •Bring your referral and identification.
  • •Remove metal from the area being examined.
  • •Tell staff if pregnancy is possible.
  • •Most routine X-rays require no fasting.
🦷

Dental imaging

  • •Remove earrings, facial piercings, spectacles and removable dentures.
  • •Bring the dentist or orthodontist referral.
  • •Remain still while the scanner rotates around the head.
  • •Tell staff if pregnancy is possible.

SAFETY FIRST

Share the right information before your scan.

MRI safety screening

MRI does not use ionising radiation, but the strong magnetic field requires careful screening.

  • •Pacemakers, aneurysm clips, cochlear implants and implanted pumps
  • •Metal fragments, surgical implants or occupational metal exposure
  • •Pregnancy, claustrophobia or need for assistance

Contrast & radiation safety

When contrast or ionising radiation is clinically indicated, protocols are selected around the real benefit to you.

  • •Report prior contrast reactions and relevant allergies
  • •Share kidney disease, diabetes and medication history
  • •Tell staff if pregnancy is possible or you are breastfeeding
Human clinical oversight: Qualified radiologists make final clinical interpretations. Results should be discussed with your referring clinician.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick, practical answers.

How long does a report take?

Routine reports are generally targeted within 24 hours and urgent cases are prioritised. Actual turnaround depends on examination type and referral urgency. Ask reception at the time of booking.

Do I need a referral?

Most imaging examinations require a referral from a doctor, specialist, or dentist so that the appropriate protocol can be selected and results reported meaningfully. Contact us if you are unsure.

Is MRI safe?

MRI uses no ionising radiation and is safe for most patients. The strong magnetic field requires careful safety screening for metal implants, devices, and pregnancy. Always complete the MRI safety questionnaire before your scan.

What payment methods and medical aids are supported?

We accept cash, card, and major medical aid schemes. Contact reception to confirm whether your specific scheme is currently on our accredited list before your appointment.

How do I access my report?

Reports are sent to your referring doctor. Copies can be collected from reception or requested by email. Visit the Patient portal for more options.

Where is the Gaborone office?

We are located at Unit 20/23, KB Mall, G-West Industrial, Gaborone. View directions on the Contact page.

Still unsure how to prepare?

Contact reception with the name of your examination and preferred appointment date. The team will confirm the correct preparation instructions.
